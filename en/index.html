What is Open-Book Pricing?

A transparent contract where every invoice discloses actual verified costs for raw inputs, manufacturing, independent QA, and delivery with zero hidden broker markups—plus a single declared +10% cost margin (= 9.09% of net invoice) locked inside an audited non-profit escrow account.
